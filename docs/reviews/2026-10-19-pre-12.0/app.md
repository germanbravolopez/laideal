# Review: app (src/app/) - release 12.0

Read-only review. Nothing built or run. Line numbers are absolute in the files as they are on `feature/aeat-direct-investigation` (HEAD 6f9c1dd).

## Summary

- Health: good, mature module with unusually strong e2e coverage (about 25 app-level e2e scenarios, UI smoke blocks B/D, `test_selfcheck`). The save, export and self-check paths are solid.
- The weak spot is the two modal Verifactu dialogs (Anular / Rectificar): they keep mutable "loaded ticket" state while an AEAT request is in flight, so a second search during the wait can make the reply mark the wrong ticket (High, 2 findings), and Rectificar can report success with no local row (High).
- Chain-integrity gate (`numberingBlockedReason`) is enforced in save / Cobrar / Rectificar / Añadir prendas but NOT in Anular or in the startup "Reintentar" (Medium-High, compliance), and there is no single-instance guard on a database that two chained-hash clients could fork (High, compliance).
- Docs: `docs/modules/mainwindow.md` is stale for the dialogs (Anular says "all rows", Rectificar says "inserts on success") and lists none of the 8 non-MainWindow files; 6 further drifts below.
- Tests: the happy paths and refusals are covered; untested are the concurrency/state cases above, Rectificar differences mode, the partial-payment block, Pendientes "Error", Anular on a ticket with a rectificativa, and the `--self-check` entry point in the installed exe.
