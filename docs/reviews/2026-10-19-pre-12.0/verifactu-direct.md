# Review: verifactu-direct (src/verifactu/aeat*, producerdeclaration)

Reviewer scope: `aeatcertificate`, `aeatdirectbackend`, `aeathash`, `aeatqr`, `aeatrecord`, `aeatresponse`, `aeatselftest`, `aeatstore`, `aeattransport`, `producerdeclaration` (+ `verifactubackend.h` interface). Read-only review, no build / no tests run.

## Summary

Health: **good, with a few compliance edges to close before production.** Hash, record XML, QR, flow control, outbox/resend, lost-reply handling and the chain sync are carefully built, XSD-validated and well tested (~60 unit cases + e2e). Main risks: (1) a cancellation whose reply was lost may be reported as ERROR on its resend, because the duplicate handling treats "invoice held as Anulada" as a failure whatever the record kind; (2) nothing ever recomputes the stored chain, although the requirements doc claims tamper detection (art. 8.1); (3) the chain after a restore can fork when the real head is a cancellation or a refused record (tracked as a GZ44 question, but there is a local mitigation: the settings marker). The investigation doc and `unit_suites.md` carry a lot of pre-13-10-2026 text (gateway hand-over) that no longer matches the code.

### Post-update self-check (`src/app/selfcheck*`)

The self-check never builds the backend, by design. Offline checks it could add on the backup copy, none of which touch AEAT:
- **Chain integrity**: `AeatChainCheck::verify(AeatStore(copy, env).recordXmls())` for both environments. Fails on any recomputed-hash or link mismatch (art. 8.1 detection).
- **Outbox age**: count of `aeat_records` still `PENDIENTE` in production and the age of the oldest. Warn when it is over 24 h, since records must be sent without delay.
- **Certificate**: when `aeat_certificate_thumbprint` is set, `AeatCertificate::loadFromStore` (read-only, local store) reports the subject, whether the NIF matches the issuer and the expiry date. Warn under 30 days.
- **Marker vs DB**: the settings' `aeat_chain_marker` vs the copy's chain head. Warn when they differ (a restored or other database: the next start needs AEAT before invoicing).
