## Current Status — October 2026

**Active branch**: `develop`

**Latest release**: [10.12](https://github.com/germanbravolopez/laideal/releases/tag/10.12) — accounting robustness and cancellation dates. **(1)** A ticket cancelled at AEAT or substituted by a rectificativa keeps its income in its payment period and is subtracted in the period of the new `ingresos.fecha_anulacion` (regularisation line + annex table), so a filed quarter never changes. Cancel/rectify mark only the paid rows the invoice covered, the ticket count is netted per period, and the dialogs refuse a cancellation while the current quarter is closed or a rectificativa dated into a closed quarter / before the original payment. **(2)** Every cancellation records `fecha_anulacion`. Local voids no longer carry `fecha_pago` / `fecha_recogida` (old ones migrated). The date is shown next to the other three in Recogida / Listado, searchable in Recogida, and an `ANULADA` row renders green. **(3)** Contabilidad figures all come from the detail rows: comma decimals are flagged uniformly, gastos-only quarters can close, the lock box is Trimestral-only, and the annual ticket count is distinct. **(4)** IVA on ingresos is a fixed 21 %. **(5)** The language setting applies live, the first run takes the installer's language, and the release notes ship in Spanish and English (installer, Ayuda, bilingual GitHub/updater body). Also in this cycle: the CI toolchain fix (MinGW 11.2), the AI-agent skills/subagents, and the tracker/milestones split.
